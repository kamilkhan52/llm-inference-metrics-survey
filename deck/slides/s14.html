14 · Same name, different quantity

One metric name, several quantities: TTFT, goodput and throughput each have rival definitions.

Papers assigned to each definition variant among the 143 full-text records available when the taxonomy was built; a paper can fall into several variants.

Variant assignments listed paper by paper in analysis/def_variants.py; counts are papers and are not mutually exclusive.

Benchmark harnesses agree that TTFT includes server-side queueing, and on TPOT, but differ on ITL, on which tokens throughput counts, and on goodput; MLPerf gates TTFT and TPOT each at P99; 11 of 13 max-rate (GP1) goodput papers use 90 %.
