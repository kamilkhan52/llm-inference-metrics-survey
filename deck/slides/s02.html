Blind checks

  • tail tiers recoded blind: 25/30, κ = 0.76
  • tail audit of 40 papers
  • 181 definition quotes verified
5

Analysis

16 metric families · Wilson 95 % CIs · scripted counts
five adversarial review rounds of the synthesis (R1–R5)

Objective: what a method is designed to improve. Constraint: what it must respect. Reported: evaluated but not targeted. Primary objectives are reliable; exact objective and constraint sets are noisier.
