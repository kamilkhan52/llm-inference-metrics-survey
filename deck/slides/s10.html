10 · Metrics over time

TTFT and TPOT/TBT targeting rose to ~45 % of our papers; E2E and throughput stayed flat.

Share of papers whose method targets each metric family (objective or constraint), by first-arXiv period; 137 full-text optimizers (34 / 57 / 46 papers).
Targeting = objective or constraint. TPOT/TBT rose as a constraint (3 % → 37 %), not as an objective (0 % → 7 %); goodput/SLO attainment is the only objective whose rise survives Holm (6 % → 30 %, Holm p = 0.03). The TTFT and TPOT/TBT rises also appear with abstract-coded papers added (8 % → 30 %, 3 % → 30 %) and point the same way within online- or mixed-load papers (unadjusted).

Why the falls are confounded by corpus mix

Model-, kernel- and parallelism-layer papers were 56 % → 37 % → 17 % of the set (no 2025–26 compression paper read in full). In the other 89 papers the falls shrink and lose significance (memory 27 % → 13 %, quality 27 % → 8 %, single-sequence speed 7 % → 5 %, all n.s.); TTFT still rises 13 % → 55 % (Holm p = 0.04).
