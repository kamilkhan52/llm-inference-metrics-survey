09 · Batch size changes the gain

In SGLang, EAGLE-3’s throughput gain shrinks from 1.81× at batch 2 to 1.38× at 64.

The abstract’s “up to 6.5×” is a single-sequence speedup over vanilla autoregressive decoding; fixed-batch throughput (no request arrivals) gains are smaller.
